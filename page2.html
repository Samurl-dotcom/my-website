Career Goals

My Vision

I hope to become an engineer who designs reliable, accessible, and high-performance web applications. My passion lies in using technology to solve real-world problems and to create tools that positively impact my community. At the same time, I am deeply committed to my journey in chess while documenting every moment with photographs with the long-term dream of becoming a Chess Grandmaster. For me, both coding and chess represent creativity, strategy, and discipline.

Focus Areas

  • Full-stack web development (JavaScript, Python, Java)
  • Backend systems and cloud computing
  • UI/UX design and accessibility
  • Data analysis and AI integration
  • Chess mastery — strategy, tournaments, and continuous improvement

Career & Personal Roadmap

Short-term (1–2 years): Complete my studies, contribute to open-source projects, sharpen my programming skills, and actively participate in local chess competitions.

Mid-term (3–5 years): Work as a full-stack developer while achieving higher chess ratings through consistent training, mentorship, and competitive play.

Long-term (5+ years): Lead development teams to create innovative solutions that have a social impact, and pursue the prestigious title of Chess Grandmaster — demonstrating excellence both in technology and on the chessboard.

Chess Inspiration

Magnus Carlsen, the World Chess Champion, inspires me through his creativity, deep calculation, and calmness under pressure. His ability to turn even the smallest advantages into victories shows the importance of patience and precision. Studying his games motivates me to keep improving and to pursue excellence both in chess and in life.
